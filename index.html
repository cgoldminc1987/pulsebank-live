Issuing writes a card and ledger entry to the connected API. It does not charge a funding source unless a supported payment flow is separately configured.

Issuing controls

Closed-loop cards follow the configured Canadian compliance rules. Open-loop Visa/Mastercard issuance is delegated to a configured issuing processor and will fail closed when unavailable.

No card is represented as funded or delivered until the API returns a confirmed result. Wallet provisioning requires the relevant Apple / Google credentials and provider setup.

Money movement workflow

  1. Verify the business and beneficial owner.
  2. Connect and verify a bank or card account with the selected provider.
  3. Obtain an authorized funding instruction and provider confirmation.
  4. Post the confirmed movement to the immutable ledger and reconcile settlement.
